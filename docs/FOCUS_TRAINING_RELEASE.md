# POWER · Focus training release

The main entry now offers **Build my focus**, with a visual goal picker, a short setup step and a reviewable session. The custom builder, all 48 progression families, yoga and ballet remain available.

## Behavior

- Personalization uses the saved working level, self-reported capabilities, equipment, inversion exit check, up to 60 days of real logs, a selected emphasis and a normal/light-day preference.
- Missing readiness is described as unconfirmed, never as a measured weakness. No training records means a clearly identified starting suggestion. No sample results or progress percentages are manufactured.
- Repeated quality results can move the suggested practice to another preparation drill within the same permitted level. A recent difficult goal-related attempt prioritizes its approved regression. Future-dated and stale attempts are excluded. A discomfort note in goal-related logs from the last 48 hours pauses launch for review; this is a conservative product rule, not a diagnosis or a scientific recovery interval.
- Light days reduce working sets. Time choices are budgets; the page also shows an approximate programmed-work/rest estimate. It does not claim a four-minute light session lasts 25 minutes.
- Every selected drill passes existing level, equipment and inversion-entry/exit rules. Preferences never promote levels or mark capabilities mastered.
- Reasons, readiness dimensions, next-path context, real technique cues, sets/repetitions/hold seconds and rest are visible or expandable. A missing exact video may show an explicitly labeled family coaching tutorial from the existing public-source ledger; it is never marked as an exact movement verification.
- Launch is in normal document flow near the overview. It requires an explicit comfort check and preserves an unfinished session unless the user confirms replacement. Coach chat remains available in the review and custom builder, with existing sign-in/quota requirements. This deterministic progression planner works without AI sign-in and is labeled as such.

## Coaching references

The decomposition into lift/compression, support and balance preparation is informed by public GMB guidance, especially https://gmb.io/press-handstand/ and https://gmb.io/planche/. The application's ranking, evidence windows and dose defaults are product rules, not universal physiological thresholds. No paid coaching material was copied.
